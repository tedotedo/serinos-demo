---

## 1. Digital Vulnerability & Brand Opportunity Audit

1. **Unclaimed UK Domain (`serinosdeli.co.uk` = `NXDOMAIN`):**
   - `serinosdeli.co.uk` is currently **unregistered** and available via Nominet registrars for ~£10/year. Securing it immediately locks in the natural `.co.uk` domain before any domain broker or competitor grabs it.
2. **Active Brand-Trust Hazard on `.com` (`serinosdeli.com`):**
   - Customers or rail travellers typing `serinosdeli.com` into their browser currently land on **Thai gambling spam**. Having an official, Google-indexed `serinosdeli.co.uk` linked on Google Business Profile, This Is Yarm, and Instagram prevents locals from stumbling onto that spam domain.
3. **Legacy Social Handle Confusion (`station5brunchandbistro`):**
   - Their Facebook URL still carries the legacy handle `station5brunchandbistro`, splitting search visibility between the old venue identity and **Serino's Deli & Wine Bar**. A dedicated web home unifies daytime brunch, deli counter, Uber Eats ordering, and evening wine/pizza service under one unmistakable brand.
4. **Unleveraged Local Proof:**
   - Serino's was voted **#1 Breakfast**, **#2 Coffee**, and **#3 Pizza** on *This Is Yarm* polls — rare daytime-AND-evening proof that currently sits buried in old social posts rather than converting searchers right beside a mainline LNER/Grand Central/Northern station.

## 4. Objection Handlers

- **"We already use Instagram / Facebook and Uber Eats."**
  - *"Those are brilliant for regulars, and the site links straight out to your Uber Eats store. The gap is Google searchers — commuters stepping off at Eaglescliffe Station, people without Instagram, or anyone typing `serinosdeli.com` and hitting spam instead of your evening wine & pizza tables. Plus direct table bookings and private hire enquiries cost 0% commission."*
- **"We don't have time to write copy or send spreadsheets."**
  - *"Zero homework. Snap a phone photo of your daytime board, pizza/wine list, and opening hours — I handle the domain registration (`serinosdeli.co.uk` in your name), setup, and real photography swap."*
- **"Our wine nights / seasonal menus change."**
  - *"Because it's clean text rather than blurry PDF uploads, updating hours or adding a festive wine night takes two minutes from a quick text message."*
